Privacy Policy

VELVET · Document set v2-2026-08 · Aligned with PDPP No. 1321/2024 principles

1. Data we collect

Phone number, profile details (name, date of birth, bios, photos, city, interests), verification images, booking and venue check-in location when you check in, chat content for moderation, payment references, and device/push tokens.

2. Why we process data

To operate invite-only membership, verify identity and age (21+), curate introductions, book partner venues, moderate prohibited content, process membership payments, and respond to safety incidents.

3. Consent

By accepting this Policy and the Terms you give explicit, informed consent to the processing described here. You may withdraw by closing or erasing your account (subject to legal retention needs).

4. Your rights

You may request access (data export), rectification via profile updates, erasure, and restriction where applicable through the app or concierge.

5. Retention

We aim to retain only what is needed. Safety, payment, and audit records may be kept longer where required by law or incident investigation. Location collected at check-in/panic is used for safety response.

6. Sharing

We share data with venue partners as needed for bookings, payment/verification processors, SMS/push providers, and authorities when legally required or for imminent safety risk.

7. Localization

Production deployments target storage of personal data in Ethiopia in line with PDPP localization requirements.

8. Breaches

We maintain procedures to assess and, where required, notify the authority and affected members without undue delay (target within 72 hours of becoming aware of a notifiable breach).

This template is not a substitute for counsel-approved Privacy Policy.

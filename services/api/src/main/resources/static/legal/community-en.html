Community Guidelines

VELVET · Document set v2-2026-08 · Adult marketplace

Respect

Treat every member with dignity. Harassment, threats, stalking, and intimidation are banned.

Adults only

Everyone on VELVET must be 21+. Adult bookings between consenting adults are the product. Involving minors is an immediate ban and report to authorities.

No CSAE

Child sexual abuse and exploitation material and related conduct are strictly prohibited and reported to authorities.

Consent and safety

No means no. Do not coerce, blackmail, or traffic anyone. Use panic and report tools if you feel unsafe.

Honest listings

Use your own photos and accurate rates/availability. Impersonation and fake verification are grounds for ban.

Keep bookings in-app

Do not push phone numbers, Telegram, or WhatsApp to evade moderation. Negotiate rates and meetups in VELVET chat.

Report

Use in-app report and panic tools. Concierge reviews safety reports promptly.
